# Echo Station art direction

Use Material-Lighting-Reference.png as the room reference. It preserves the central deck, stepping platforms, western relay, eastern log room, and the 11:11 silhouette. Compare from the entry vista and the east threshold.

- Industrial blue-black joints and mid-toned brushed steel establish depth. Keep material highlights tight at machined edges, with rougher oxidised joints.
- Warm amber belongs to the west relay stairs and burner. Cool cyan identifies the east log room. Keep both smaller than the clock's silhouette.
- Give the basin long, low ripples and directional reflections. Local outlets break the surface. Keep retained wake and reduced-motion behavior.
- Place fire, sparks and startup fragments at believable machinery sources. Preserve the jump gaps, contrast of the landing plates, and visibility of the relay.
- Treat the image as a lighting and material target. The in-engine captures measure the actual result.
