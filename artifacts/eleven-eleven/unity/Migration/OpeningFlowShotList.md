# 11.11 — افتتاح Google Flow

يبدأ مسار Unity بتشقق الشاشة بعد اكتمال الغلاف. يتبعه خروج إيكو من المدرسة، وصوله إلى المختبر، أسئلة التجربة، التجربة، ذكرى المدرسة، ثم واجهة النظام والاستيقاظ في Sector 11. يبقى التكسّر وواجهة النظام داخل Unity حتى يتبعا الحركة المخفّضة والإيقاف.

## التوليد

Omni، أفقي 16:9، مخرج واحد لكل لقطة. افحص رصيد الحساب قبل الإرسال. ابدأ بلقطة المدرسة بطول 8 ثوانٍ. ثبّت الوجه والشعر والزي فيها قبل توليد بقية اللقطات. صور اللقطات القديمة مراجع للهوية والإخراج، والمقاطع النهائية تأتي من Flow.

### 1. مغادرة المدرسة — 8 ثوانٍ

Reference — Echo - school.png

Hand-painted theatrical 2D anime. Match Echo's face, messy black hair and simple black school uniform from the ingredient, including the same age and proportions. Late afternoon in a Japanese school foyer. Begin on the hand sliding the glass exit door open, then follow at shoulder height as Echo walks from the school through the gate toward the quiet street. Blue school-window reflections, natural warm daylight, restrained acting and one continuous stable camera move. End with Echo passing a dark doorway edge that can cut to the lab entry. No wing, cape, weapon, powers, dialogue, or text. Ambient footsteps and door sound only.

### 2. دخول المختبر — 8 ثوانٍ

Continue the exact Echo design and uniform. Start behind Echo as the laboratory door slides open, then take a calm slow dolly into the cool clinical room. His father in a white lab coat and glasses gestures for him to approach the examination chair. Show Echo's hesitant look, the father's reassuring body language, clean practical lights and a readable room layout. Leave a steady wide two-shot at the end for Unity's dialogue. Theatrical 2D anime, no spoken dialogue, generated text, violence, wing or powers. Soft door and ventilation sound only.

### 3. التجربة — 6 ثوانٍ

References — approved lab clip Echo frame, and Echo - experiment.png.

Keep the same Echo and clinical lab geometry. Echo reclines in the exam chair, closes his eyes, and breathes steadily. His father taps the console; diagnostic pulses settle into a stable repeating rhythm. Move in slowly toward Echo's closed eyes. Lab light falls gently into indigo as a reflection of a sunny school window becomes visible for the memory cut. Use precise restrained acting, simple non-invasive sensors, no violence, no transformation, no wing, no dialogue, no readable text and no flashes.

### 4. ذكرى المدرسة — 4 ثوانٍ

References — approved Echo frame and Echo - school memory.png.

A brief warm memory with the same Echo in his original school uniform alongside the two classmates in the ingredient. Framed at eye level from three-quarter profile. Echo's eyes soften as his friends turn toward him in late afternoon light; soft tree shadows move across their shoulders. A brief, gentle fade through dark indigo at the end for the Unity system reveal. Match their recognizable faces and the theatrical 2D anime style. Keep the shot quiet, no readable text, no dialogue and no new story event.
