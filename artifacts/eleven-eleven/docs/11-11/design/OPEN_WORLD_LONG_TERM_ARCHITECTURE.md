## Approved Japanese-city direction (Owner revision 2026-09-19)

Setting: **Minato-Kasumi** (Owner-approved name, 2026-09-20), a fictional Japanese
coastal city inspired by Yokohama's waterfront,
rail-connected neighborhoods, residential hills and port/research contrast.
Do not copy its map or substitute a generic neon-anime city. The approved name is
locked; detailed geography and history remain design decisions, not invented Canon.
Candidate districts: station/commercial core, residential-school cluster,
waterfront, historic streets and medical/industrial edge. Include only areas that
earn their gameplay, relationships and mystery; no city assets are commissioned now.

The first future slice connects hospital and exterior, nearby street, residential
block, bus stop, konbini and the route toward home. Home/school/station expansion
waits for this compact cluster's acceptance. Rail/public transport
supports believable routines before any large vehicle simulation. Authored NPC
schedules connect home/work/school and remember relevant player actions.
Research street markings, signage, crossings, drainage, bicycle parking and
ordinary interiors when producing the actual district; this is not a tourist list.

Day: warm everyday life. Evening: social/nostalgic. Night: quiet rail sounds,
water reflections and restrained unease. Weather and narrative state may change
presentation without making every street a glitch. Sparse anomalies (an impossible
train time, a changed alley, a contradictory memory) invite theories, not immediate
confirmation of the false exit. Preserve player controls and accessible UI truth.

- Districts: small authored clusters with a distinct daily purpose, social bond
  and secret; no vast empty map. Release one walkable neighborhood first.
- Home: emotional refuge and evidence of absence. Cooking/cleaning/planting only
  if they support choice or relationships, never compulsory daily chores.
- School: friendship, pressure, clues and Echo's chess; no attendance grind.
- Phone: authored contacts/messages/photos/maps/schedule; later System interference.
  Never a permanently flashing task list.
- NPCs: authored states + bounded schedules + relationship/event memory. Optional
  AI dialogue reads a lore-safe projection; cannot grant rewards or invent Canon.
  No live LLM per NPC per frame.
- Interiors: A authored meaningful room; B reusable kit with authored variations;
  C plausible temporary access limit. Doorbells can yield no answer, refusal,
  conversation or invitation. Do not promise every building is enterable.
- Vehicles: bicycle → selected motorcycle/car only after traversal/streaming/
  collision evidence. No GTA-scale simulation or physics investment now.
- Relationships: remembered actions and authored responses; warmth before threat.
  Preserve Yuki/Shizuka identity and ambiguity; no affection purchase treadmill.
- Shops: believable services/cosmetics after authority/economy gates. No paid
  randomness, scarcity pressure or acquisition systems in the current phase.
- Secrets: ordinary human detail → relationship clue → System anomaly → Zero
  echo → Kinja evidence → false-reality question. Not every secret glows.

Only protect cheap irreversible boundaries now: stable asset IDs and provenance,
unit scale/axes, ownership of save/progression, spoiler-safe content references,
localization/accessibility. Do not create managers, streaming frameworks or NPC
simulation because they might be useful later. The accepted vertical slice is
the prerequisite, not an obstacle to this future.
