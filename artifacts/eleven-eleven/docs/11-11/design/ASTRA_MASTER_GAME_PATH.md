## North star and player fantasy

I am Echo: I can trust my observation, but not every interpretation the System
gives me. I want to escape without abandoning what makes me human. A small act
of remembering must visibly change what I can do, not merely add archive text.
Quality benchmarks: control/readability/cohesion/character performance, not
asset counts, resolution labels or promises to exceed another game wholesale.

## Three signature candidates — design hypotheses, not playtest scores

| Candidate | Fun / originality | Canon / emotion | Cost / replay / expansion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Witness Anchor — تثبيت الأثر** | Choose which witnessed version of a local place the System must honor; navigation tradeoff rather than filter toggling | Strong memory/false-reality fit; preserving evidence can cost a safe route | Medium; two authored local states, one anchor; reusable in puzzles/threats/dialogue |
| Eleven-second Echo | Replay a recorded route to coordinate machinery with one's past motion | Time/11:11 fit, but implies an early supernatural ability unless clearly system-mediated | High replay and engineering cost; risks familiar clone puzzles and tight-input frustration |
| Contradiction Ledger | Cross-check objects, voices and UI claims to challenge one false System instruction | Excellent investigation and intimacy; weaker moment-to-moment physical play | Low-medium; broad narrative reuse, risks menu evidence matching |

**Choose Witness Anchor**, with Contradiction Ledger as feedback, not a second
large system. Initial prototype: safe contained room, two observed versions of
one conduit/partition, only one may be stabilized. One restores a passage; the
other keeps a personal recording audible. The player can preview and undo for
free; completion requires deliberately using the chosen world state. This is
System machinery responding to observed evidence, not Echo's Zero power.

Teaching: observe discrepancy → compare two states → anchor one → walk the
changed route → hear/see the consequence → restore/try the alternative. No
random flicker, unseen collision switching, precision countdown or punishment
for accessibility settings. Never close geometry through the player. Required
prototype evidence: both routes reachable, repeat/reset safe, choice explained
by environment, one player can predict consequences. If removing anchoring
leaves the task unchanged, reject it. **Not implemented or fun-validated yet.**

## Loop and 28-minute definitive slice

Observe → test a hypothesis physically → choose/anchor → act under manageable
risk → recover a human memory → world responds → new question. Repeat in
5–10-minute variations, not inspect/read/inspect/read.

Target **28 minutes**, acceptable 20–35 after real playtests. Start with a
cohesive 3–5-minute opening repair; do not pad the room to hit a duration.
Cover reconstruction → fracture → brief experiment transfer → playable wake
→ third-person exploration → 11:11 → first witnessed contradiction → anchor
puzzle → one failed-subject threat/escape → relief → next-sector hook.
No full Zero, Kinja torture, open city or boss tree in this slice.

Inside-system campaign target after slice acceptance: **minimum 90 minutes,
ideal 120, review ceiling 180**. These are editorial budgets, not current
content estimates or a promised release length. Expand only for meaningful
new verbs, attachment and consequences. See INSIDE_SYSTEM_COMPLETE_FLOW.

## Narrative and progression contracts

- Quests progress from observe → compare → manipulate → evade → decide; every
  objective provides a reason, discoverable action, feedback and next question.
- Early combat: shove, improvised defense, evasive movement and environmental
  escape only when the encounter earns them; no casual power fantasy. Later
  Zero makes speed/perception/control meaningfully different and costly.
- Memories change a route, a response or an available inference; no mandatory
  lore wall. Relationships begin through Echo's small human choices, not meters.
- Kinja is measured paternal/scientific control, not shouting villain exposition.
  Yuki/Shizuka pressure tests trust and memory, not a cheaply scored death choice.
- Zero: implication only → despair → black ocean → intimate contract → player-
  participated transformation → consequence. Do not implement those beats now.
- System UI: cold clinical labels, concise next action, later contradictory
  claims; keep the player's control/accessibility settings honest.
- Companion: prototype persistent / contextual / system-bound apparition with
  the same clue. Default **contextual**, silent unless useful. Test comprehension,
  interruption and solitude; identity/origin remain unrevealed and provisional.
- Wish/false exit → hospital → empty home → school → Yuki/Shizuka → anomaly.
  Do not tell the player the answer immediately. Outside life must matter even
  if its ontology is uncertain. Preserve Echo's chess hobby.
