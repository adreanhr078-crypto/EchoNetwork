## Five mandatory pillars

1. **Chromatic rendering.** Forward+ is the reference tier: HDR3D, MSAA4x, screen-space AA; ACES exposure1.18, white6.5; saturation1.38, contrast1.22, brightness1.04. Use volumetric fog with anisotropy0.7, height variation, multilevel bicubic bloom threshold0.85, SSR, SSAO and SSIL. Keep readable faces, objectives and navigation. Explicit lower tiers and unsupported-renderer fallbacks reduce effects with a recorded reason; do not claim Forward+ acceptance from Compatibility.
2. **Custom materials.** Visible geometry uses authored ShaderMaterials. Convert remaining StandardMaterial3D slots through the shared adapter, preserving textures, normal/ORM channels, UV transforms, vertex colors, alpha/culling, emission and material sharing. Preserve existing purpose-built shaders. Characters/props use three-band chromatic cel, view Fresnel and light-masked rim, colored wrapped subsurface edge and anisotropic/iridescent highlights. Surfaces use depth/parallax, roughness/reflection response, rune/caustic layers, emission masks and player proximity. Keep energy flow off for ordinary skin/cloth and before earned power.
3. **Kinetic camera.** Route gameplay through DynamicActionCameraRig with critically damped follow/predictive look-ahead, SpringArm collision, speed lens70..92, restrained bank1.5..3.5deg, directional multiband trauma and landing spring. Preserve public orbit, touch/mouse, lock-on and cinematic suspend/return ownership. Reduced Motion disables bank/shake/lens pumping/blur. A camera must never move through a wall for a visual effect.
4. **Movement and secondary motion.** Use frame-rate-independent exponential acceleration/braking and procedural lean/recoil on presentation nodes. Existing authored rolls/root motion/ledge contacts own their phases. Never add another bob/IK/scale that breaks foot or hand contact. Wind/velocity drives secondary spring helpers on expressly eligible accessories; never rewrite Golden or automatically override protected V31 bones. Validate any cape driver against the actual garment before enabling it.
5. **Reactive VFX.** Pool GPU bursts, footstep surface ripples/dust, sprint wind, dash/weapon ribbons and landing response; three depth layers of ambient micro-life follow the active camera. Trigger steps from real animation contact signals, never a second timer. Use physical dust/air/surface response before the pact; emissive magic follows earned gameplay state. Effects serve action and atmosphere without hiding cues. Freeze local clocks and provide static feedback for Reduced Motion, honor pause/mute and measured tier budgets.
